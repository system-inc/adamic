The refusal visits the identifier inside a parenthesized argument separately.
Climb through its enclosing parentheses to find the argument's call, and compare
ast.SkipParentheses(argument) with that identifier before asking the existing
nodeHostConsumesArgument(call, index). Direct arguments and non-node relations
retain their existing behavior. No library predicate or lowerer was modified.

structural views refused with double parentheses at eleven sites: initializer,
