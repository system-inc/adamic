## Rulings supplied by @system_adamic

Step 41: a read-only walk that only reads node references and never stores or returns them needs no retain or release, proven by borrow inference.

Step 44: a value read from a container or field is borrowed only when (1) its owner provably outlives the read, (2) no write or call that might overwrite/remove the slot occurs in the borrow span unless proven not to, and (3) it is not stored, returned or captured. Otherwise it is an ordinary counted read. The compiler proves this; there are no hand annotations or new syntax.

The hand experiment preserved original owned-return APIs and all child-field ownership. Its lexical filter is not a production proof of lifetime or mutation. Compiler inference must prove all three conditions, including callbacks. This handoff asks for count acceptance, not a speed guarantee.
