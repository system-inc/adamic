# 99-apple-visit-nodes-guard: the reviewer's mutations of the output

Each row is a reviewer's mutation of what the adapter writes: one of the six edits dropped, or a check moved after
its return. Its patch changes the adapter to write that output, one line after the edits are made, and moves
`pins.json`'s after owner and its hash to match, so the apply still passes and `test.cjs` reads the mutated
`-after` ref. Every row turns `TestSixEditsInPlace` red, and most also `TestOrderOfChecks` and `TestVisitNodesLoops`.
`cloud/gates/mutants-red.sh` runs a row by its name, `stage3/adapt/99-apple-visit-nodes-guard/test.mutants.md:<row>`.
