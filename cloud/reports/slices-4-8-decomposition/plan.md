## Known slice-8 reds and the 60-second law

The source is `a9296157:cloud/reports/slice-8c2/report.txt`, read in full. These observations were reproduced by that report on its pre-8c2 compiler containing `a17e41a5`; they are inherited blockers, not proof that an 8c2 merge introduced them. This plan did not rerun them on main or C7.

- R1: `async_methods.a` counted/split build has conflicting `adamic_method_async_methods_a_Worker_work_771fdf0e0a64745c66e078ad537670ab` declarations. Also blocks graph counts/oracle.
- R2: `entries_record_alias.a` C emission rejects initializer declarator `adamic_temporary_module_entries_record_alias_a_ddcd6df0207e425d751a647822065b57_85`.
- R3: `program_region/tsc_node_membership.a` panics in checker.GetTypeArguments via graphFlows.
- R4/R5: `review/agree/fxspptb_iterators_derived_symbol.a` and `fxspptb_iterators_override_source.a` panic through Node.Text on ComputedPropertyName in checkMemberOverrides.

The broad native Program/Region/Shape/Split selection passed in 10.764s after `07dc6cfb`; this says nothing about cold products on a new cut. The broad oracle selection failed in 80.598s; the lower selection timed out at 90.017s; excluding TestCyclesCorpus passed in 12.462s. Counts regeneration failed in **58.62s**, already close to 60 even before repair. Corpus regeneration failed in **316.435s**. The source left counts unchanged from `285d7ef9` (1,090 row occurrences) and cycles.json at the selected integration recording (1,728 entries). No successful refresh was recorded.

Every queue **test and product**, including setup/build/training products, must individually finish within 60 seconds. A 90s/600s outer limit, package total, cached run, or subprocess hidden behind a wrapper is not compliance. `U` in the table means current leaf/product duration is unknown, so it is not certified below or safely far from 60. Owners must measure cold and warm products, split ownership where needed, and keep the same Node/ASan/UBSan/leak/count assertions. Historical TSan 82s, moves WASI 99s and record-mutant 117s from the c2 plan remain inherited cautions; C7 has since changed their harnesses, including an explicit large-WASI move skip, so do not reintroduce old aggregations or claim those reds fixed here.
